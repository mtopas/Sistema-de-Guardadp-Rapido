Progressive Web App (PWA) — this is the move for you right now.
  - It's a website that behaves like an app: installable from the browser, works on phone home screen, can share content to it via the native share sheet.
  - No App Store. No review process. No $99/year Apple fee.
  - Your FastAPI + a good frontend already gets you 80% there.

  Hosting from home:
  - Technically yes — run the server on your PC, expose it with a tool like Cloudflare Tunnel (free, no port forwarding, no static IP needed).
  - But: your home internet going down = app goes down. Your PC sleeping = app goes down.
  - Better: a cheap VPS (DigitalOcean, Hetzner) costs ~$5/month and stays on 24/7.

  Realistic path:
  PWA frontend → deploy backend on a $5 VPS → share via Cloudflare or nginx →
  users install it from the browser like an app

  You skip the stores entirely, it's public on the internet, and people can install it on Android or iPhone from Safari/Chrome. Android especially treats PWAs as first-class    
  citizens.
